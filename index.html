Research

lncRNA expression under cold and drought stress in conifers

Umeå University · MSc thesis · in submission

Confirmed long non-coding RNA loci in Pinus sylvestris and Picea abies don't come with clean functional annotation the way protein-coding genes do. My approach assigns each lncRNA a functional proxy — the nearest protein-coding gene within 100 kb — then tests those neighbour gene sets for GO and KEGG pathway enrichment across needle and root tissue, under cold and drought stress.

Nearest-neighbour proximity is a functional proxy, not evidence of regulatory function — a distinction the thesis is explicit about throughout.

The pipeline runs end-to-end on a SLURM HPC cluster: alignment with minimap2, interval work in bedtools, enrichment in R/Bioconductor (topGO), with a coordinate liftover step for the spruce genome. It produces the comparative visualisations — UpSet plots, heatmaps, bubble plots — used throughout the thesis.

minimap2 · bedtools · R/Bioconductor · topGO · SLURM · Micromamba

Combined CYP3A4/CYP3A5/ABCB1 polymorphisms in tacrolimus dosing

MAHE Dubai · 30 ECTS thesis, course 5MO134 · extraction phase

A systematic review and meta-analysis asking a narrower question than most of the existing literature: do combined CYP3A4, CYP3A5, and ABCB1 genotypes predict tacrolimus pharmacokinetics better than single-gene analyses alone, across kidney, liver, heart, and lung transplant populations.

128 studies extracted so far from a post-deduplication pool of 1,300 records (2,732 identified across PubMed, Scopus, and Embase). Title and abstract screening is next, followed by a random-effects meta-analysis with subgroup analysis by organ type and genotype combination.

PRISMA · Zotero · R (meta-analysis)
