Research

lncRNA expression under cold and drought stress in conifers

Umeå University · MSc thesis · in submission

Conifers like Scots pine and Norway spruce dominate boreal forests precisely because they've evolved robust ways of coping with cold and drought — and long non-coding RNAs are increasingly implicated as part of that regulatory toolkit. The trouble is that, unlike protein-coding genes, confirmed lncRNA loci don't come with clean functional annotation. My approach works around that by assigning each lncRNA a functional proxy — the nearest protein-coding gene within 100 kb — and testing those neighbour gene sets for GO and KEGG pathway enrichment across needle and root tissue, under cold and drought stress conditions.

Nearest-neighbour proximity is a functional proxy, not evidence of regulatory function — a distinction the thesis is explicit about throughout.

The pipeline runs end-to-end on a SLURM HPC cluster: alignment with minimap2, interval work in bedtools, and enrichment analysis in R/Bioconductor (topGO), with a coordinate liftover step to extend the method from pine to the spruce genome for cross-species comparison. It produces the comparative visualisations — UpSet plots, heatmaps, bubble plots — used throughout the thesis to compare stress response patterns across tissues and species.

Combined CYP3A4/CYP3A5/ABCB1 polymorphisms in tacrolimus dosing

MAHE Dubai · 30 ECTS thesis, course 5MO134 · systematic review underway

Tacrolimus has a notoriously narrow therapeutic window, and clinicians already know that CYP3A4, CYP3A5, and ABCB1 genotypes each shift how patients metabolise it. What's less settled is whether looking at these genes together actually improves dose prediction over the single-gene analyses most of the literature still relies on. This systematic review and meta-analysis is built to answer that directly, pooling pharmacokinetic outcomes — trough concentration and dose-adjusted ratios — across kidney, liver, heart, and lung transplant populations.

The project follows PRISMA methodology from the ground up: literature search across PubMed, Scopus, and Embase, deduplication and screening in Zotero, structured full-text data extraction, and a prior-meta-analysis and narrative-review mapping exercise to place the work against what's already been published. Full-text extraction is well underway, with title/abstract screening and the random-effects meta-analysis — including subgroup analysis by organ type and genotype combination — still ahead.
