Research

lncRNA expression patterns in Pinus sylvestris and Picea abies under abiotic stress

Where
Umeå Plant Science Centre, Umeå University
Type
MSc thesis, 30 ECTS
Supervisor
Nathaniel Street
Status
In submission

Long non-coding RNAs are well established as regulators in herbaceous plants but poorly characterised in conifers, even though Scots pine and Norway spruce meet cold and drought across the boreal forest, in needles and roots that do very different jobs. My thesis maps where lncRNA loci sit in each tissue under each stress, in both species.

Candidate lncRNAs were called for each sample group, aligned to merged reference transcriptomes, and sorted by which samples share each locus. The pine design crosses stress (cold, drought) with tissue (needle, root), and the spruce dataset adds somatic and zygotic embryo samples. Most of these lncRNAs are novel and have no annotation of their own, so I gave each locus a functional context from its nearest annotated protein-coding gene within 100 kb, then tested those neighbour gene sets for GO and KEGG enrichment.

Nearest-gene proximity is an inference, not a validated regulatory link, and the thesis says so throughout.

  1. Alignminimap2 (splice preset) to merged reference transcriptomes, on SLURM
  2. Comparebedtools multiinter: conserved, tissue- and stress-specific loci
  3. Filter and assignIntersect with curated lncRNA sets, then nearest gene within 100 kb
  4. EnrichGO with topGO, KEGG with a hypergeometric test, on eggNOG-mapper annotations

eggNOG assigns terms through mostly animal and fungal orthologs, so human-disease pathways can swamp a conifer analysis. I filtered those out of the KEGG background, along with animal-specific and xenobiotic pathways, and removed non-plant GO terms.

The whole workflow runs in Snakemake on a SLURM HPC cluster with pinned Micromamba environments, so pointing it at another species means editing paths and sample names rather than code. It is open source on GitHub.

Do CYP3A4, CYP3A5 and ABCB1 genotypes together predict tacrolimus exposure?

Where
Umeå University
Type
Systematic review and meta-analysis, 30 ECTS
Supervisor
Dr. Naushad Rais, MAHE Dubai
Status
Report in preparation

Tacrolimus has a narrow therapeutic window, and genotype shifts how much of it ends up in a transplant patient's blood. CYP3A5 is the well-known player. What's less settled is whether adding CYP3A4 and the ABCB1 transporter improves on a CYP3A5-only view. This project pools dose-adjusted trough concentrations (C0/D) across kidney, liver, heart and lung recipients to test that.

Search and selection. I searched PubMed, Scopus and Embase under PRISMA 2020, then deduplicated and screened the records in Zotero, first on title and abstract and then on full text. Studies were eligible if they reported tacrolimus levels or dose by genotype in solid organ transplant recipients for at least one of six variants: CYP3A5 rs776746, CYP3A4 rs35599367 and rs2242480, and ABCB1 rs1045642, rs2032582 and rs1128503. I also went through the reference lists of earlier meta-analyses to catch studies the databases missed.

Extraction and quality. I read the full texts and extracted genotype-stratified trough concentrations and doses, along with organ, age group, ancestry and time point, into a structured sheet with one row per genotype group and time point. Study quality was scored with the Newcastle-Ottawa Scale, and overlapping cohorts were flagged so they could be dropped in a sensitivity analysis.

Statistics. Within each study, genotype groups were compared on log C0/D, then pooled in a multilevel random-effects model in R (metafor, REML) with study as the random effect and cluster-robust confidence intervals. I ran subgroups by organ, age group, post-transplant period and ancestry, plus leave-one-out and sensitivity analyses and exploratory small-study checks. Studies reporting combined CYP3A4, CYP3A5 and ABCB1 genotypes were assessed separately from the single-gene models.

In silico. I also annotated the six variants for splicing, regulatory and expression effects, so the pooled estimates can be read against what each variant is predicted to do.

Reproducibility. The analysis code is open source. One command runs the models, the sensitivity and ancestry analyses, the figures and the summary tables, and it ships with a synthetic example dataset.
